# The Realms of Arcana — sourced art direction

## Goal
Create an original 78-card fantasy-realistic Tarot deck for OBus. The deck uses historical Tarot symbolism and compositional lessons without reproducing any historical card, contemporary illustration, protected fantasy character, costume, logo, or artist-specific style.

## Online references consulted

1. The Morgan Library & Museum, **Visconti-Sforza Tarot Cards**
   https://www.themorgan.org/collection/tarot-cards
   - Fifteenth-century hand-painted aristocratic deck.
   - Transferable principles: precious-material hierarchy, heraldic frontal staging, luminous gold fields, highly legible costume silhouettes.

2. Wikimedia Commons, **Pierpont Morgan-Bergamo Visconti-Sforza Tarot**
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Pierpont_Morgan-Bergamo_Visconti-Sforza_Tarot
   - Public-domain scans used for direct visual study.
   - Lovers: two grounded figures beneath a supernatural figure; triangular hierarchy; brocade fabrics; shallow ceremonial stage.
   - Star: one monumental figure, dominant celestial emblem, minimal landscape, blue/red/gold material contrast.

3. British Museum, **Sola-Busca Knight of Swords**
   https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1845-0825-485
   - The museum describes the 1491 series as the earliest complete surviving true Tarot and notes precise outline, regular parallels, and cross-hatching.
   - Transferable principles: strong anatomy, forceful contrapposto, oversized weapon silhouette, engraved value modeling, narrative court cards.

4. Wikimedia Commons, **Sola-Busca tarot deck**
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Sola-Busca_tarot_deck
   - Public-domain historical deck.
   - Transferable principles: active Minor Arcana figures, martial fantasy costume, object-driven storytelling, dense hatch texture.

5. Sacred Texts, **The Pictorial Key to the Tarot**
   https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/
   - Symbolic descriptions and the complete 78-card illustrated narrative tradition.
   - Transferable principle: every Minor Arcana card is an emotional event rather than a repeated suit pattern.

6. Wellcome Collection, **The Tarot pack of cards — 78 prints after watercolours by Pamela Colman Smith**
   https://wellcomecollection.org/works/vvhjuc5a
   - Confirms the full structure: 22 Major Arcana and four suits of 14 cards.

7. British Museum, **Tarot de Marseille pack**
   https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1904-0511-47-1-78
   - Transferable principles: unmistakable suit emblems, bold silhouette, economical color separation, visual consistency.

8. Contemporary process references (composition only; no image or style copying)
   - Book of Adeptus: https://bookofadeptus.ryanginter.com/
   - Natalia Weedy Tarot campaign: https://www.nataliaweedy.com/projects/tarot-campaign
   - Transferable principles: one coherent world, vertical cinematic staging, photographed/material realism, environmental storytelling, distinct character cast.

## Original visual system

### Shared rendering
- 720 × 1120 WebP paintings.
- Cinematic fantasy realism with believable proportions.
- Layered atmospheric perspective: foreground figure, midground event, distant realm.
- Directional rim light, volumetric haze, metal/fabric/stone differentiation.
- Painterly glazing and controlled grain rather than flat vector geometry.
- Ornate but restrained frame; title and suit/rank remain readable.

### Major Arcana
Twenty-two individually staged mythic scenes. Each uses a unique environment, pose, celestial condition, dominant symbol, and color script.

### Suit realms
- **Wands — Emberwild:** volcanic sunset, charcoal forests, copper armor, firelight, motion and ambition.
- **Cups — Tidemoon:** moonlit coast, blue glass, silver cloth, reflections, intimacy and memory.
- **Swords — Stormhold:** wind-carved citadel, steel plate, rain, cold skylight, conflict and intellect.
- **Pentacles — Verdant Crown:** mountain gardens, mossy ruins, bronze/gold, harvest light, craft and material life.

### Rank storytelling
- Ace: singular emblem revealed by divine light.
- Two: choice, duality, and horizon.
- Three: formation, cooperation, or consequence.
- Four: enclosure, stability, or pause.
- Five: disruption and conflict.
- Six: passage, exchange, or victory.
- Seven: strategy, defense, or assessment.
- Eight: movement, craft, binding, or acceleration.
- Nine: culmination under pressure.
- Ten: completion, burden, or inheritance.
- Page: youthful messenger.
- Knight: armored action.
- Queen: sovereign interior mastery.
- King: mature outward command.

## Copyright boundary
Historical public-domain decks inform symbolism and general composition. Contemporary sources inform only high-level production lessons. No card reproduces a reference figure, layout, costume, logo, or a living artist’s distinctive treatment.
